## Main theorems (`WightmanToAlgebra/Main.lean`)

```lean
theorem wightman_to_haagKastler (T : WightmanTheory H Labels) (hT : T.Axioms) :
    (polynomialNet T hT).Isotony ∧
    (polynomialNet T hT).EinsteinCausality ∧
    (polynomialNet T hT).Covariant (polynomialCovariance T hT)

theorem yangMills_haagKastler (hYM : JointExistence) :
    ∀ g : GaugeGroup, g.IsSimple → ∃ C : WilsonFamily g, WilsonRegularity C ∧
      ∀ p : ℕ → ℕ, StrictMono p → ∃ q : ℕ → ℕ, StrictMono q ∧
        ∃ T : Hierarchy C, T.Properties (p ∘ q) ∧
          ∃ hax : T.theory.Axioms,
            (polynomialNet T.theory hax).Isotony ∧
            (polynomialNet T.theory hax).EinsteinCausality ∧
            (polynomialNet T.theory hax).Covariant (polynomialCovariance T.theory hax)
```

`P(O)` is the unital subalgebra of `End(D)` generated by smeared fields `φ(l, f)` with
`tsupport f ⊆ O`; the star is the formal adjoint on the Wightman domain `D`;
`polynomialCovariance` is `X ↦ U(g) X U(g)⁻¹` with `U(g) = onStates (coordinates.symm g)`.
`JointExistence` is a hypothesis because importing the upstream proof (`Solution.lean`)
builds the full construction; the upstream `YangMills.jointExistence` discharges it.

The Wightman vacuum `ω(X) = ⟨Ω, XΩ⟩` is a Poincaré-invariant state satisfying the spectrum
condition (FR Def. 24, via vector-state spectral measures supported in the closed forward
cone) with `Ω` the unique translation-invariant vector (FR Def. 25), and every GNS
representation of it is unitarily equivalent to the Wightman fields acting on `A(M)Ω ⊆ D`.

## Blockers (`BLOCKERS.md`)

- **B1 Timeslice.** False for polynomial nets of general W0–W3 theories (generalized free
  field with continuous Källén–Lehmann measure; sketch in `TIMESLICE.md`). The upstream
  construction exports no field equation or other dynamical relation; the von Neumann
  routes need unbounded spectral theory absent from Mathlib.
- **B2 GNS space vs `H`.** The Wightman GNS space is `closure(A(M)Ω)`; proving it is all of
  `H` needs joint continuity of Wightman functions in the Schwartz topology
  (Banach–Steinhaus on Fréchet spaces), not done.
