## What is claimed

1. `WightmanToAlgebra.wightman_to_haagKastler`: any Wightman theory satisfying the
   upstream axioms W0–W3 (`.lake/packages/ym_core/PublicTheory.lean`) gives a net of
   local unital *-algebras (polynomial algebras of smeared fields on the Wightman domain)

   "Algebraic Quantum Field Theory – an introduction", arXiv:1904.04051, Sec. 4.1.

Fidelity (the important part):
